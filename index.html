01Who is included?Counts & intervals +

Only episodes with at least 28 days of follow-up after MRI enter the audit. The waiting-time comparison uses the subset with a biopsy within 28 days and both intervals recorded. A shorter average can hide people still waiting: always read the counts alongside it.

Included episodes and descriptive measures
MeasureBeforeAfter

“Pending”, “not planned” and “unknown” describe status at extraction. No biopsy planned is not a negative cancer diagnosis. A zero-day interval means the same calendar day only if the source uses calendar-day differences.

03How the audit worksMethod & sources +

A deliberately narrow question

For each period, calculate the mean of MRI-to-report and report-to-biopsy intervals on the same complete episodes. Their sum equals the mean MRI-to-biopsy interval. Compare after minus before.

The fixed 28-day observation window is a prototype design choice, not the NHS Faster Diagnosis Standard, a clinical target or a NICE requirement. Longer waits remain visible in the counts, but do not enter the interval chart.

Median and 90th percentile are also shown. Quantiles use linear interpolation. No significance test, risk adjustment or causal estimate is supplied. Rounding to one decimal can hide differences smaller than 0.05 days.

What this cannot establish

Restricting analysis to completed biopsies creates selection bias. This is an exploratory audit, not proof that AI works, saves lives, avoids biopsies or is cost-effective. It does not measure histology turnaround or time to diagnosis. Review locally before any service use.

Column definitions & preparation
period
before or after. Use consecutive, comparable cohorts at one site.
biopsy_status
completed, pending, not_planned or unknown at extraction. Do not treat missing outcomes as no biopsy needed.
mri_to_report_days
Days from MRI to the agreed clinical report milestone. Blank if unavailable. Use the same definition in both periods.
mri_to_biopsy_days
Days from MRI to biopsy. Only for completed biopsies; blank if missing. Must not precede the report.
followup_days
Days from MRI to the extraction cutoff, even if biopsy occurred earlier. Required for every episode. Less than 28 days is excluded.

Calculate intervals inside your approved environment before exporting. Use one row per episode, deduplicate locally and remove identifiers. This tool cannot detect duplicate episodes. Decimals are allowed; unknown values must be blank, not zero. Use a decimal point and comma separators.
