# Explain After MRI in your own words

## One sentence

“I built a small audit tool to check whether faster prostate MRI reporting gets patients to biopsy sooner, or whether the wait just moves downstream.”

## A 30-second demonstration

1. Open **Delay moves**. The blue reporting segment shrinks from about four days to one.
2. The later wait grows from six days to nine. Both totals remain ten days. These are invented examples, not Lucida results.
3. Open **Pathway improves**. The reporting gain now translates into a shorter total.
4. Open **Follow-up incomplete**. It withholds the comparison because the after cohort is too immature.
5. Download the note: it records the denominators, missingness, follow-up and caveats automatically.

## If Antony asks why it is relevant

“I saw that your NHS pilots are evaluating the wider diagnostic pathway. I wanted to make one small part of that evaluation easier to inspect and repeat. I don't know what you already use internally, so I'd value the clinical team's feedback on whether this is useful.”

## What the numbers mean

- The chart shows mean days among completed biopsies within 28 days, with enough follow-up and both intervals recorded.
- The denominator is not every referred patient. Pending and unobserved outcomes are kept visible separately.
- The 28-day window is our prototype choice. It is not a NICE requirement or the Faster Diagnosis Standard.
- A difference before and after implementation is not proof that AI caused it.
- A biopsy taking place is not the same as a confirmed diagnosis.

## Be straightforward about the build

“I used AI to help research, design and implement it. The workflow, code, assumptions and tests are public. I'd like to work under supervision on evidence review and evaluation, and adapt it to a real team's needs.”

Do not claim independent validation, a partnership with Lucida, access to NHS data, measured time savings, diagnostic performance or guaranteed clinical benefit.

## A sensible next ask

“Would a small part-time clinical research project—evidence extraction, data definitions or checking an evaluation dataset—be useful to your team?”

Do not promise specialist engineering support or clinical responsibility you cannot deliver.
